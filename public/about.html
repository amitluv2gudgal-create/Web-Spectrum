About BLACK CAT ERP

BLACK CAT ERP is a powerful and intelligent Security Services Management ERP specially designed for security guard agencies, manpower companies, facility management organizations, and multi-branch service enterprises.

Developed under the leadership of Cdr. (Retd.) Omprakash Rai, BLACK CAT ERP combines real operational expertise with modern SaaS technology to deliver a complete business automation platform for security operations, payroll, billing, compliance, attendance management, reporting, and workforce deployment.

The ERP is designed to eliminate manual paperwork, reduce payroll errors, improve operational transparency, and simplify day-to-day administration for manpower-driven businesses.

Advanced Payroll Engine

BLACK CAT ERP includes an advanced payroll engine capable of handling complex salary structures across multiple clients, categories, designations, and branches.

Features include:

  • ✔ Overtime Calculation
  • ✔ PF / ESI / PT Deductions
  • ✔ Advance Recovery
  • ✔ Uniform Deductions
  • ✔ Mess Deductions
  • ✔ Salary Slip Generation
  • ✔ Payroll Locking System

Security & Data Protection

BLACK CAT ERP follows strong SaaS security architecture with organization-level data isolation, secure authentication, role-based permissions, and protected middleware systems.

The system is carefully designed to prevent cross-organization data leakage and maintain complete payroll and billing integrity.
